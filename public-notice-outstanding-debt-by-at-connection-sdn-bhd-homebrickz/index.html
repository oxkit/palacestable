Public Notice

Outstanding Debt by AT Connection Sdn Bhd (Homebrickz)

A formal notice from Palace and Stable PLT regarding non-payment by AT Connection Sdn Bhd

Background

AT Connection Sdn Bhd, operating under the name Homebrickz, is a short-term rental operator in Malaysia, primarily managing Airbnb and Booking.com properties. The company is owned by Tan Tee Kung (also known as Addison Tan). Palace and Stable PLT supplied mattresses to AT Connection Sdn Bhd, and initially, payments were made promptly. However, since November 2023, AT Connection Sdn Bhd has consistently delayed payments, citing partnership issues as an excuse. Despite occasional small payments following reminders, the company has failed to settle the outstanding amount.

Notably, Addison Tan Tee Kung, acting on behalf of 183 Leisure Resort Sdn Bhd as a minor shareholder, placed orders to be billed under 183 Leisure Resort Sdn Bhd in an apparent attempt to avoid responsibility for the debts incurred.

As of the date of this notice, AT Connection Sdn Bhd and 183 Leisure Resort Sdn Bhd collectively owe Palace and Stable PLT a total of RM 41,764 for goods supplied.

Court Judgment Details

On April 15, 2025, the Magistrate's Court in Kuala Lumpur issued a summary judgment (Civil Suit No. WA-A72NCC-40521-09/2024) in favor of Palace and Stable PLT against AT Connection Sdn Bhd and 183 Leisure Resort Sdn Bhd. The court ordered the following:

  1. AT Connection Sdn Bhd and 183 Leisure Resort Sdn Bhd are jointly liable to pay RM 6,264.
  2. AT Connection Sdn Bhd is solely liable to pay RM 35,500.
  3. Interest at a rate of 5% per annum on the amounts of RM 6,264 and RM 35,500, as per Section 11 of the Civil Law Act 1956, from the date the debts were incurred.
  4. Costs of RM 2,500 to be paid by the defendants.

The judgment was issued by Magistrate Muhamad Taufik bin Saberi through the e-Review platform, following an application filed on November 10, 2024.

Call to Action

Palace and Stable PLT urges AT Connection Sdn Bhd and its owner, Addison Tan Tee Kung, to settle the outstanding debt of RM 41,764 with interest and legal costs immediately to avoid further legal action.

We also caution other businesses to exercise due diligence when dealing with AT Connection Sdn Bhd or Homebrickz due to their history of delayed payments, non-compliance with court orders, and attempts to evade responsibility through actions such as billing under 183 Leisure Resort Sdn Bhd.

For inquiries or further information, please contact Palace and Stable PLT through our legal representative, Raqib Osman, at lawyer@raqibosman.com.my.
