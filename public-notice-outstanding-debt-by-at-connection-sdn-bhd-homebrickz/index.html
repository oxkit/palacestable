02Background背景

AT Connection Sdn Bhd, operating under the name Homebrickz, is a short-term rental operator in Malaysia, primarily managing Airbnb and Booking.com properties. The company is owned by Tan Tee Kung (also known as Addison Tan). Palace and Stable PLT supplied mattresses to AT Connection Sdn Bhd, and initially, payments were made promptly. However, since November 2023, AT Connection Sdn Bhd has consistently delayed payments, citing partnership issues as an excuse. Despite occasional small payments following reminders, the company has failed to settle the outstanding amount.

AT Connection Sdn Bhd（以 Homebrickz 名义经营）是马来西亚一家短租运营商，主要管理 Airbnb 及 Booking.com 房源。该公司所有人为 Tan Tee Kung（亦称 Addison Tan）。Palace and Stable PLT 曾向 AT Connection Sdn Bhd 供应床垫，初期对方均能及时付款。然而，自 2023 年 11 月起，AT Connection Sdn Bhd 以合伙问题为借口持续拖延付款。尽管经多次提醒后偶有零星小额付款，该公司始终未结清欠款。

Notably, Addison Tan Tee Kung, acting on behalf of 183 Leisure Resort Sdn Bhd as a minor shareholder, placed orders to be billed under 183 Leisure Resort Sdn Bhd in an apparent attempt to avoid responsibility for the debts incurred.

值得注意的是，Addison Tan Tee Kung 作为 183 Leisure Resort Sdn Bhd 的小股东，以该公司名义下单并要求将账单开至 183 Leisure Resort Sdn Bhd 名下，明显企图逃避其债务责任。

As of the date of this notice, AT Connection Sdn Bhd and 183 Leisure Resort Sdn Bhd collectively owe Palace and Stable PLT a total of RM 41,764 for goods supplied.

截至本公告发布之日，AT Connection Sdn Bhd 与 183 Leisure Resort Sdn Bhd 就所供货物合计拖欠 Palace and Stable PLT 共计 RM 41,764。

03Prior Court Judgment Involving Tan Tee KungTan Tee Kung 涉及的前案判决

The name Tan Tee Kung appears in a published academic study on directors' liabilities under Malaysian law (Separation Rule for the Director and the Company: Malaysian Law Position, International Journal of Research and Innovation in Social Science, DOI: 10.47772/IJRISS.2025.909000646). The paper discusses the High Court case Bester Malaysia Sdn Bhd v Tan Tee Kung & Ors [2025] MLJU 2441, in which the company sued Tan Tee Kung - its own director and chief executive officer - for breach of trust, breach of fiduciary duties and negligence over two business deals in which the company lost RM 3,472,504.40.

Tan Tee Kung 的名字出现在一篇关于马来西亚法律下董事责任的已发表学术研究（《Separation Rule for the Director and the Company: Malaysian Law Position》，国际研究与创新社会科学期刊，DOI：10.47772/IJRISS.2025.909000646）中。该论文讨论了高等法院案件 Bester Malaysia Sdn Bhd v Tan Tee Kung & Ors [2025] MLJU 2441：其任职公司起诉 Tan Tee Kung（该公司董事兼首席执行官）违反信托、违反受信责任及疏忽，涉及两宗使公司损失 RM 3,472,504.40 的商业交易。

The Court held that Tan did not exercise independent judgment as sole director and CEO, and failed to meet the standard of reasonable care, skill and diligence required of directors under the Companies Act 2016. He was ordered to pay damages assessed at RM 2,822,504.40, plus interest and costs of the action.

法院裁定，Tan 作为唯一董事兼首席执行官未行使独立判断，未达到 2016 年公司法令对董事合理谨慎、技能与勤勉的标准，被判赔偿 RM 2,822,504.40，另加利息及诉讼费用。

This prior multimillion-ringgit judgment forms part of the publicly documented court record involving Tan Tee Kung and company funds under his control.

这一涉及数百万令吉的前案判决，构成 Tan Tee Kung 在其掌控公司资金方面的公开法院记录的一部分。

04Court Judgment法院判决

On April 15, 2025, the Magistrate's Court in Kuala Lumpur issued a summary judgment (Civil Suit No. WA-A72NCC-40521-09/2024) in favor of Palace and Stable PLT against AT Connection Sdn Bhd and 183 Leisure Resort Sdn Bhd. The court ordered the following:

2025 年 4 月 15 日，吉隆坡推事庭作出简易判决（民事诉讼案号：WA-A72NCC-40521-09/2024），判 Palace and Stable PLT 胜诉，AT Connection Sdn Bhd 及 183 Leisure Resort Sdn Bhd 败诉。庭令如下：

PartyLiabilityAmount 当事方责任金额
AT Connection Sdn Bhd + 183 Leisure Resort Sdn Bhd Jointly liable连带共同承担 RM 6,264
AT Connection Sdn Bhd Solely liable单独承担 RM 35,500
Interest利息 5% per annum on RM 6,264 and RM 35,500, per Section 11 of the Civil Law Act 1956, from the dates the debts were incurred 根据 1956 年民事法第 11 条，RM 6,264 及 RM 35,500 自债务发生之日起按年利率 5% 计息 accruing持续累计
Costs诉讼费用 Payable by the defendants由被告承担 RM 2,500
Total principal adjudged判决本金合计 Plus interest and costs另加利息及诉讼费 RM 41,764

The judgment was issued by Magistrate Muhamad Taufik bin Saberi through the e-Review platform, following an application filed on November 10, 2024. The defendants have not complied with the order.

该判决由推事 Muhamad Taufik bin Saberi 通过 e-Review 平台作出，相关申请于 2024 年 11 月 10 日提交。被告至今未遵守该庭令。
